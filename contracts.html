Define shared business records, projections, aggregates, and pure functions used by client and server modules. Group related data by business concept.

Models neither obtain external capabilities nor perform observable effects. They must not import server infrastructure, SvelteKit request APIs, or component code. Repository-specific SQL, transaction mechanisms, and operation parameters stay in repositories; use-case dependency/parameter/result types stay beside their operation.
